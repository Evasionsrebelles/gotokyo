About

A map designed for independent travellers

Go Busan lists Busan's points of interest on an interactive map. Every place comes with a detailed listing: description, category, neighbourhood, admission and a link to the official website. Built by travellers, for travellers.

Features

What the app offers

🗺️

Interactive map

Browse all of Busan's tourist sites on a smooth map with real-time geolocation. Tap a marker to instantly open the place listing.

📋

Complete place listings

Each place displays its Korean name, category, neighbourhood, description, admission price, opening hours, practical info and a photo.

🔍

Advanced filters

Combine multiple criteria at once to narrow your search. Only the places matching all selected filters appear on the map.

🚇

Integrated metro network

Display metro stations directly on the map, line by line (Lines 1 to 4, Donghae Nambu, LRT and Hubs). Perfect for planning your journeys.

Place details

All the information at a glance

Tapping a map marker instantly opens a detailed popup: the place name in English and Korean, photo, category, neighbourhood, description, admission price and practical info — all in one place.

Save your favourites by bookmarking any place you are interested in and find them again later.

When a place has an official website and/or online ticketing, dedicated buttons appear in the popup and open the page directly in the app's built-in browser.

Launch your selected places instantly in your preferred navigation app. Go Busan is compatible with Naver Map, KakaoMap and Google Maps so you can get directions and find your way around Busan with ease.

Advanced filters

Find exactly what you are looking for

The filter panel combines multiple criteria simultaneously. Only places matching every selected criterion are shown.
