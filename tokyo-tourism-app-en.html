Features

What the app offers

🗺️

Interactive Map

View all Tokyo's tourist sites on a smooth map with real-time geolocation. Click on a point to instantly display the place details.

📋

Complete Place Listings

Each place displays its name in Japanese, category, neighborhood, description, price, hours, practical information and a photo.

🔍

Advanced Filters

Refine your search by combining multiple criteria simultaneously. Only places matching your criteria appear on the map.

⭐

Favorites and Hotspots

Save your favorite places and discover community-recommended hotspots for an optimal sightseeing experience.

Place Details

All information at a glance

Click on a map point to instantly open a detailed popup: place name in English and Japanese, photo, category, neighborhood, description, price and practical information. Everything is in one place.

Save your favorites by bookmarking all the places that interest you for later reference.

When a place has an official website and/or online ticketing, dedicated buttons appear in the popup and open the page directly in an integrated browser within the app.

Instantly display selected places in your preferred navigation app. The app is compatible with Google Maps to get directions and navigate easily during your Tokyo visit.

Advanced Filters

Find exactly what you're looking for

The filter panel combines multiple criteria simultaneously. Only places matching all selected criteria are displayed.
