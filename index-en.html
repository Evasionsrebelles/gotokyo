Japan

A country where tradition and modernity meet

Japan is an East Asian archipelago of 125 million people whose capital, Tokyo, is one of the most dynamic megacities in the world — blending shrines, temples and skyscrapers in perfect harmony.

From national parks to ultra-modern cities and well-preserved traditional neighbourhoods, Japan offers a remarkable diversity of landscapes and experiences.

Tokyo, Japan

The megacity of a thousand faces

Japan's capital with 14 million inhabitants, Tokyo is a sprawling city famous for the Shibuya crossing, the skyscrapers of Shinjuku, the Senso-ji temple in Asakusa and its colourful districts such as Harajuku and Akihabara.

Host to countless cultural festivals throughout the year, it is also the ideal starting point for exploring the surrounding regions.

From street food and traditional public baths to a rich cultural scene and varied landscapes, Tokyo is an authentic city with an incredible range of things to discover.
