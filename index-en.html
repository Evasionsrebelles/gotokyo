South Korea

A country where every street tells more than one story

South Korea is an East Asian peninsula of 51 million people whose capital, Seoul, is one of the most dynamic megacities in the world — blending palaces, temples and skyscrapers in perfect harmony.

From national parks to ultra-modern cities and well-preserved hanok villages, Korea offers a remarkable diversity of landscapes and experiences.

Busan, South Korea

A coastal metropolis with many faces

South Korea's second city with 3.4 million inhabitants, Busan is a port city famous for its urban beaches at Haeundae and Gwangalli, the Jagalchi fish market — the largest in the country — and its colourful hillside neighbourhoods.

Host city of the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF), it is also the ideal starting point for exploring the coast and surrounding mountains.

From street food and traditional public baths to a rich cultural scene and varied landscapes, Busan is an authentic city with an incredible range of things to discover.
